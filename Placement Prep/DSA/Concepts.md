
## Time Complexity

The time it takes for an algorithm to finish

![468](Pasted%20image%2020260904213257.png)

- O(1) : Constant time complexity. Lowest
- O(n) : Linear time complexity. Depends on a single variable. Increases alongside n
- O(Logn) : Logarithmic time complexity. Less than O(n). *Eg. Binary search*
- O($n^2$) : Depends on two factors (Double loops)
- O($n^3$) : Depends on three of factors
- O($2^n$) : Time taken multiplies by two every time input increases ( *powers of 2* ). Eg.  **Fibonacci sequence using recursion** 
- O(n!) : Factorial time complexity.
